Nice to meet you.

This is Yuu's little corner of the internet. After school at Yagen East Academy — the window seat of the library, the club room, the register of the bookstore where I work — these pieces of everyday life will slowly be written down here, one by one.

## Why start writing

The reason is simple: after reading so many other people's stories, I suddenly wanted to see what it would feel like to write something down myself.

They say "love" is a feeling that needs translating. Until I learn to translate it, I'll start by recording every ordinary day.

## What you'll find here

- Reading notes (mostly shoujo novels, with occasional personal biases)
- Small records of academy life and the student council
- Ideas that don't have a name yet

If one day these words become a story in someone else's heart — that would be wonderful.

Please take care of me.
